# Jump played evidence controls

`tools/check_jump_controls.py --route <forward-extracted-route.json> --project-root <saved-project> --godot <console.exe> --output <new-directory>` runs the saved Player and AnimationTree at 30, 60 and 120 FPS. The route must contain four distinct successful public cases. `--checker` can select an isolated absolute checker script; its `res://` helper imports still resolve from the supplied saved project.

The positive run must pass before the combined receipt can pass. Seven negative runs must detect their named failure in every Cartesian matrix cell: tiny hip hop, delayed physical apex, unrecovered rotations, doubled and reversed actor travel, penetrating ankle, and a single-key knee discontinuity. Both AnimationLibrary and Animation are copied before mutation. Scene hashes are checked before each load and again after all controls. No saved clip is modified.

Apex height is played hip displacement relative to its initial pose. The height allowance is the greater of 0.002 leg lengths and gravity times the squared playback-plus-authored sampling interval. Apex timing allows two playback frames plus one authored sampling interval. Flight onset/end use simultaneous ankle height above 0.01 leg lengths; the tolerance additionally accounts for crossing that band at ballistic launch velocity. The declared height, apex and contact events must also agree with a 9.81 m/s^2 ballistic flight. Final recovery compares every bone in skeleton space transformed into world metres, removing only declared unextracted forward travel. Actor transport cannot hide arm or torso residuals.

Grounded extraction is measured from the applied world displacement, not parent-local delta magnitude. The absolute 10 leg lengths/s and 50 rad/s discontinuity limits remain unchanged. Reports include sampled peak-speed differences at 60/120 FPS as diagnostics; no averaging or convergence ratio can turn a failing absolute speed into a pass. Contact, penetration and knee-pole gates remain mandatory. Numerical checks do not establish visual approval or skinned sole collision.

Validation at implementation: Godot 4.7.2 check-only and Python compilation pass. A smoke test using the preserved old forward baseline detected knee discontinuity in all 24 plays with zero engine errors; that baseline still fails its pre-existing missing reach/physical timing contracts. The repaired public candidate positive and complete corruption matrix remain pending root integration.
